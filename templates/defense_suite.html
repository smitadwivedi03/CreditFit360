Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Statutory Consumer Protection Framework

Under Section 21(3) of CICRA 2005 and RBI Circular RBI/2023-24/72, credit bureaus (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF) and lenders must rectify disputed records within 30 calendar days. If they fail to resolve the dispute within 30 days, they are legally mandated to pay you compensation of ₹100 per calendar day of delay without requiring court proceedings.
