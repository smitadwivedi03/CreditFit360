About CreditFit360

CreditFit360 is an educational financial-health assessment project designed to help users understand important financial indicators through simple tools and interactive simulations.

Our Purpose

Financial decisions can be difficult when income, expenses, existing EMIs, credit usage and savings are considered separately.

CreditFit360 brings these indicators together in one place to help users explore their financial position and understand areas that may need attention.

This application is an educational project. It does not represent a bank, lender, credit bureau or regulated financial institution.

Our Approach

We aim to make financial concepts easier to explore through accessible language, understandable calculations and a simple user interface.

The application's scores are illustrative outputs from its own formulas. They are not official credit scores or guarantees of creditworthiness.
